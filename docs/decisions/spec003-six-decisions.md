### Controlled amendment — customer enrollment OTP (Owner, 2026-10-30)

Owner replaces only the customer **enrollment verification** link24h with a six-digit email OTP valid for 120 seconds, at most five attempts per proof, and resend under existing email/IP quotas. Resend revokes the prior proof. The email carries an opaque proof locator, not the verifier; the customer must type the OTP. Existing 24h links issued before this amendment remain consumable until their original expiry. Pending enrollment may be stored without authority for delivery, replay defense and expiry; `identity.user_account` and login authority are created only after a valid proof is atomically consumed. Expired OTP does not activate an account. Password recovery retains its purpose-bound 15-minute link and session invalidation. Vietnamese domestic phone input may be canonicalized to `+84` contact metadata; unverified phone is never login or recovery authority. All other IAM-CUSTOMER-01 controls remain in force.

Account-first business model and Spec-first → Spec-by-Spec → Full-stack End-to-end Delivery → Full Spec Acceptance remain settled. Phone authentication remains a future verified-channel dependency when that target is introduced; no unsupported phone login/recovery is claimed by the initial email channel.
